# Privacy Policy

At **davidalvarezp.com**, we take the protection of your personal data very seriously. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your data in compliance with **GDPR** and **Spanish data protection laws (LOPDGDD)**.

## Data We Collect

- Name and email when you send a contact message.
- Browsing data through cookies and analytics.

## Purpose of Data Processing

- To handle contact requests.
- To improve website functionality and user experience.
- To analyze website usage for statistical purposes.

## Legal Basis

Data processing is based on your consent when sending data or accepting cookies.

## Data Recipients

We do not share your data with third parties except as required by law or with service providers who help us manage the website.

## User Rights

You can exercise your rights of **access, rectification, deletion, objection, restriction, and portability** by sending an email to **contact@davidalvarezp.com**.

## Data Retention

Data will be retained as long as necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or to comply with legal obligations.
